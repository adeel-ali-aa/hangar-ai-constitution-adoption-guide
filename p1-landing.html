What is the Hangar AI Constitution?

The Hangar AI Constitution is a set of 168 laws that govern how AI agents write, review, and evolve code at American Airlines. When your team adopts it, every pull request is reviewed by a constitutionally-governed agent — consistently, every time — against the same engineering, product, and business rules.

This guide gives you one URL and one path: from "what is this?" to your team's first constitutionally-governed pull request. Pick your role below to get started.
